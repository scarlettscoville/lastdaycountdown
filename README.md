# lastdaycountdown
A countdown to the last day of school.

## How to use

1. Open `index.html` in your browser.
2. The timer counts down to May 19, 2026 at 3:15 PM Eastern Time (America/New_York).

## Details

- Target date: **May 19, 2026 at 3:15 PM ET**
- The app updates every second and displays days, hours, minutes, and seconds remaining.
